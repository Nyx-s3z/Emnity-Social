WELCOME TO EMNITY SOCIAL

Community Rules

Before you get started, please read the rules below. You must agree to them to continue.

  1. No Sexual Harassment

    Sexual harassment of any kind is prohibited. Violations may result in a 7–14 day ban.

  2. No Racism or Religious Discrimination

    Discrimination or hateful behavior toward someone because of their race, skin color, or religion is prohibited. Racism may result in a 40-minute to 1-day ban. More serious cases involving religion may result in a 1–3 day ban.

  3. No Staff Targeting (Staff Only)

    Staff members must not repeatedly target and ban one person without a valid reason. Violations may result in demotion or removal from the staff team.

  4. No Cyberbullying

    Deliberately targeting someone to upset, harass, or bully them is prohibited. Violations may result in a 1-hour to 2-day ban.

  5. No Malicious Content

    Sending or sharing malicious content is prohibited and may result in a permanent ban.

  6. No Spamming

    Repeatedly spamming or disrupting the community is prohibited and may result in a 5–15 minute ban.

Moderation

Server moderation stays in each server chat. Open a server and use the Mod view toggle above the Members panel to show Ban, Unban, and 10-minute Timeout actions. Direct messages do not have moderation controls.

Site-wide account moderation is restricted to Dev accounts. Server moderators can only moderate members of their own server.
